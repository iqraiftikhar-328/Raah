## Eligibility
For FAST undergraduate computing/engineering admissions, the official eligibility criteria require at least 60% in SSC and 50% in HSSC, with the required Mathematics background. Applicants may use the FAST-NUCES test, SAT, or NTS NAT route as specified by FAST.

## Fees
FAST states a tuition rate of PKR 12,000 per credit hour from Fall 2026. FAST does not publish a single fixed semester tuition amount because semester cost depends on registered credit hours; additional admission, security and Student Activity Fund charges also apply.

## Deadlines
For the 2026 undergraduate cycle, FAST listed 20 May–30 June 2026 for application submission, with admission tests from 29 June–10 July 2026 and merit-list announcement on 22 July 2026.
