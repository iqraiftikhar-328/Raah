## Eligibility
Bahria University Islamabad E-8 lists Bachelor of Laws as a four-year undergraduate programme. Applicants should verify the current BUET/admission-test and Pakistan Bar Council/HEC requirements in the official admission material.

## Fees
For Fall 2026/Spring 2027, Bahria lists LLB at PKR 11,680 per credit hour; 17 first-semester credit hours give tuition of PKR 198,560 plus PKR 15,000 miscellaneous charges, total PKR 213,560 per semester. One-time admission fee is PKR 33,000 and refundable caution money is PKR 25,000. The fee page notes the programme is subject to NOC of Pakistan Bar Council/HEC.
