## Eligibility
Air University requires at least 50% in HSSC/equivalent with Mathematics for BS Computing programmes. Students without Mathematics may have to complete the stated Mathematics deficiency courses.

## Fees
For Fall 2026, Air University lists BS Computer Science at PKR 9,150 per credit hour and 15 first-semester credit hours, giving tuition of PKR 137,250. Allied charges include PKR 2,500 application processing, PKR 20,000 admission fee, PKR 5,000 endowment fund, PKR 6,000 miscellaneous charges, and PKR 10,000 refundable security deposit.

## Deadlines
For Fall 2026, Air University listed 2 July 2026 as the application deadline for Phase-II, III & IV undergraduate programmes. The official schedule also lists Islamabad CBT dates in June/July 2026.
