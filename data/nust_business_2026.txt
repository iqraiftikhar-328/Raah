## Eligibility
NUST lists BBA under Business Studies & Social Sciences. The academic-qualification page shows BBA is available to several FSc/FA/A-Level subject combinations. The official undergraduate admission information states minimum 60% in completed HSSC and 60% in SSC for general UG eligibility.

## Fees
For national students, 2026-27 tuition for Social Sciences & Business Studies is PKR 302,950 per semester, plus PKR 5,000 miscellaneous charges. One-time admission processing fee is PKR 35,000 and refundable security deposit is PKR 10,000. BBA also has an HBS licensing fee of PKR 15,000 per year.

## Deadlines
For the 2026 cycle, the official UG portal listed 25 July 2026 as the ACT/SAT application-form deadline. NET-2026 Series-4 had an 18 June 2026 application deadline. The 2026 admission cycle is now in its selection-list stage; students should verify the next cycle on the official portal.
