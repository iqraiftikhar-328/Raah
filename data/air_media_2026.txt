## Eligibility
Air University lists BS Communication & Media Studies. Its eligibility page states applicants need Intermediate Part I and II with at least 50% marks.

## Fees
For Fall 2026, Air University lists BS Communication & Media Studies at PKR 6,500 per credit hour and 18 first-semester credit hours, giving tuition of PKR 117,000. Allied charges include PKR 2,500 application processing, PKR 20,000 admission fee, PKR 5,000 endowment fund, PKR 6,000 miscellaneous charges, and PKR 10,000 refundable security deposit.
